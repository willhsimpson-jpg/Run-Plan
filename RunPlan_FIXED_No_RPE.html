How the plan works

Week 0 (Oct 11–17) is a lead-in before the plan starts on Oct 18.

Race: half marathon on Saturday, April 3, 2027 (week 24). Predicted finish about 1:40 (7:38/mi), range 1:37 to 1:44. Weeks 21–22 add half-pace work to the long run, week 23 is the taper, week 24 is race week, and week 25 is recovery.

Four runs a week through week 26: Mon and Tue easy, Thu workout, Sat long run. Easy runs build from 45 to 60 min, the long run from 75 min to 2 hours. Strides after easy runs aren't listed since you already do them.

From week 27 (Apr 18, 2027) Monday becomes a second workout and Friday adds a 60 min easy run. Weekly miles stay under the 50 mile cap (peak 49.7). Every workout has a 2 mile warmup and 2 mile cooldown at easy pace.

Lifting follows your split: Sun legs, Mon push, Tue pull, Wed legs, Thu push, Fri pull, Sat rest. Each lift is one checkbox with the exercises shown under it.

Weekly score = points earned ÷ points available that week. Workouts and the long run are 3 points, easy runs 2, lifts 2, and the race 5. A score of 90+ is a full week. The average only counts weeks where you've checked at least one item.

Every 4th week is a cutback with a zone-2 checkpoint: run 30 min at one fixed heart rate and log the pace in that week's checkpoint box. Paces are predictions. If you're 10+ sec/mi slower than the slow end of the predicted zone-2 range on two checkpoints in a row, repeat the last 4 weeks before moving on. Run hard days by effort if the pace is off.

Sunday is legs, so a hard Monday run follows a leg day, and Wednesday legs sit before Thursday's workout. Swap days if it feels like too much.
